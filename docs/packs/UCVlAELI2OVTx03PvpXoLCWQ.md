# Iran, China, Fascism, and Boycotting FIFA | Ajamu Baraka & Dimitri Lascaris

**Channel:** BettBeat Media  
**Published:** 2026-07-19  
**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Phvnx5x56y8

> **The Gist** — BRICS nations, driven by capitalist structures and elite interests, often prioritize global economic stability over ideological anti-imperialism, as seen in China's oil market interventions.

## Summary

This segment analyzes the geopolitical friction between China's market-stabilizing actions and Iran's strategic use of oil as an economic weapon against Western hegemony. Following the outbreak of conflict, China significantly reduced its oil imports, which, alongside massive releases from the US Strategic Petroleum Reserve, kept global oil prices unexpectedly low. While some analysts attribute this to China's concern for the Global South or tactical hedging to avoid extreme Western retaliation, the speakers argue that these actions expose the limits of ideological solidarity within the anti-imperialist bloc.

Ajamu and Dimitri Lascaris contend that observers must evaluate these states by their material outcomes rather than rhetorical stances. They highlight a persistent "naivety" in Russian and Chinese foreign policy, which often pursues "peaceful coexistence" with a Western imperialist system that is fundamentally committed to maintaining its global hegemony. This naivety is structurally linked to the domestic political economies of BRICS nations.

Ultimately, the discussion concludes that BRICS members—including China, Russia, India, and Brazil—are fundamentally capitalist societies ruled by economic elites. While they challenge US unilateralism, their state actions are heavily influenced by domestic oligarchs and the preservation of global capitalist markets, meaning they cannot be viewed as pure or reliable anti-imperialist actors.

## Highlights

- China's dramatic reduction in oil imports after February 28, combined with US strategic reserve releases, prevented a predicted spike in global oil prices.
- Analysts suggest China's market stabilization may be a "slow boil" strategy to avoid triggering extreme, potentially nuclear, reactions from the US and Israel.
- The speakers critique Russian and Chinese foreign policy for a "strange naivety" in expecting rational, peaceful coexistence with Western imperialism.
- BRICS nations are characterized not as ideological saviors, but as capitalist states whose foreign policies are compromised by domestic billionaire elites.
- Iran's internal political landscape remains divided between neoliberal moderates seeking Western integration and radicals pushing for hardline resistance.

## Key Concepts

- **Materialist Analysis** — Evaluating geopolitical actors based on the physical and economic consequences of their actions rather than their stated ideological intentions.
- **BRICS Capitalism** — The reality that major emerging economies operate as capitalist systems with powerful oligarchies, limiting their commitment to genuine global anti-imperialism.
- **Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR)** — Government-controlled fuel stocks used to manipulate market supply and suppress oil prices during geopolitical crises.

## Why It Matters

This analysis demystifies the BRICS bloc, showing that global multipolarity is shaped more by competing capitalist interests and elite preservation than by cohesive anti-imperialist ideology.

# One Empire, Three Wars: Africa, the Sahel, and Iran — with Gerald Horne & Kit Klarenberg

**Channel:** BettBeat Media  
**Published:** 2026-09-05  
**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WJ4PyHqU3Ao

> **The Gist** — The panel discusses the decline of Western imperial influence in Africa and West Asia, highlighting the rise of multipolarity and the proxy roles played by countries like Ukraine.

## Summary

The panel analyzes the precipitous decline of U.S. and French imperial influence across Africa and West Asia, arguing that Western powers are increasingly relying on desperate, covert tactics to maintain control. Journalist Kit Klarenberg highlights the Pentagon's launch of "Drongo Company," a new psychological operations unit in Africa, as well as France's reliance on Ukraine as a military proxy. Ukraine is reportedly marketing its battle-tested drone warfare and assassination capabilities to Western powers, actively assisting Al-Qaeda-affiliated groups in Mali to destabilize revolutionary governments.

Professor Gerald Horne contextualizes these developments historically, explaining the post-WWII division of imperial labor where France maintained economic hegemony via the CFA Franc while the U.S. provided strategic backing. However, this arrangement is collapsing due to popular anti-imperialist movements in the Sahel (Mali, Burkina Faso, Niger) supported by Russia and China. Horne also notes the contradictions in U.S. foreign policy, such as using African nations as dumping grounds for deportees while simultaneously attempting to counter Chinese economic dominance.

The speakers conclude that the era of Western unipolarity is ending. This transition is marked by the consolidation of alternative security and economic frameworks, such as the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), and the rise of regional powers like Iran and China, which are successfully resisting Western sanctions and military pressure.

## Highlights

- The Pentagon has launched "Drongo Company," a new psychological operations unit aimed at influencing African audiences amid declining Western prestige.
- Ukraine is acting as a sub-imperial proxy for France in the Sahel, reportedly training Al-Qaeda-affiliated militants in Mali in drone warfare.
- The Alliance of Sahel States (Mali, Burkina Faso, Niger) represents a significant anti-imperialist shift, backed by Russian and Chinese security cooperation.
- The historical French economic hegemony in West Africa via the CFA Franc is rapidly disintegrating as local populations reject neo-colonial structures.
- The expansion of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) demonstrates the growing integration of Central Asia and the Global South away from U.S. hegemony.

## Key Concepts

- **Drongo Company** — A newly established U.S. military psychological operations unit designed to conduct deceptive influence campaigns across the African continent.
- **CFA Franc** — A French-controlled colonial currency system established post-WWII to maintain economic dominance and resource extraction in West and Central Africa.
- **Sub-imperial Proxy** — A secondary state actor that executes military or strategic objectives on behalf of a dominant imperial power in a foreign theater.

## Why It Matters

This shift signals the collapse of post-colonial Western hegemony in Africa and the Middle East, accelerating the global transition toward a multipolar world order.
